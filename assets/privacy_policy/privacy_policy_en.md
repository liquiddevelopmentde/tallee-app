## 2. Data Processing

Tallee operates primarily offline. Your game data, including players, groups, games, matches and statistics, remains on your device. We do not have access to this data.

Data is transmitted only when you use a feature that requires transmission:

- **Online sharing:** When you enable online sharing, the selected match data and the IP address associated with the connection are transmitted to our server in the European Union. The data is automatically deleted after no more than ten minutes. This feature is disabled by default and is activated only after your explicit confirmation.
- **Error reports:** In the event of a technical error, technical information may be transmitted to our error-reporting service GlitchTip in the European Union. This may include the error message, app version, operating system, device model, time of the event and IP address. The IP address may be stored as part of the relevant error event and may be visible in GlitchTip. This data is processed to analyze errors and improve the stability and security of the app and is deleted after no more than 90 days.
- **Feedback:** If you voluntarily submit feedback, your message and, where provided, your name and email address are transmitted to our feedback service in the European Union. This data is deleted after no more than 90 days.

Data transmissions use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for personalized advertising or user profiling.

## 3. Legal Bases

We process data on the following legal bases:

- **Online sharing:** Your consent pursuant to Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.
- **Voluntary feedback, including voluntary name and email details:** Your consent pursuant to Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.
- **Error reports:** Our legitimate interest pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in analyzing errors and maintaining the stability and security of the app.

You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. The lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal remains unaffected.

## 4. Recipients, Service Providers, Third Countries and Retention

We use technical service providers for hosting, error reporting and feedback processing. They process data solely on our behalf and according to our instructions. They are contractually required to provide an appropriate level of data protection.

The service providers we use provide a level of protection equivalent to the protection described in this Privacy Policy and required by applicable law.

We generally do not transfer data to countries outside the European Union or the European Economic Area. Where Apple or Google process data in connection with app distribution or app-version checks, this is done under their applicable privacy terms and, where required, on the basis of appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses or an applicable adequacy decision.

The retention periods are:

- local game data: until you delete it from your device;
- online-sharing data: no more than ten minutes;
- error reports: no more than 90 days;
- feedback: no more than 90 days;
- technical server and security logs: no more than 90 days, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Data may be deleted earlier when it is no longer required for the relevant purpose.

## 6. Users Outside the European Union

Tallee is available worldwide. Users outside the European Union are additionally protected by the mandatory data protection rights of their respective country.

Where the law of your country provides additional rights or safeguards – for example in the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Brazil, California or other jurisdictions – we will comply with those requirements to the extent legally required.

We do not sell personal data and do not share personal data for targeted or personalized advertising.

## 7. No Tracking or Advertising
